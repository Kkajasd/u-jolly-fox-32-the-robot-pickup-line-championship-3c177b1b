Entry for the make.fun bounty **The Robot Pickup Line Championship**: write one pickup line, a single line of at most 200 characters, in the voice of a robot trying to charm someone. An AI judge compares entries pairwise on robot-ness, whether the joke grows out of being a machine, surprise, economy, and charm.

> I asked my autocomplete to finish this sentence, and it just typed your name.

The joke is rooted in being a machine: autocomplete predicts what comes next, and here the machine's most likely completion is a person's name — the robot's crush leaking out of its own prediction engine. Short on purpose: economy is one of the five judging weights.

## How it works

Run `node main.ts` (Node 22+). It writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` with no trailing newline, then verify with:

```sh
cat /output/entry.txt
```
